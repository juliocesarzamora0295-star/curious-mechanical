03 — Maintenance

The part that decides what the other two cost

Installation happens once. Service happens when it is already too late.

One loop, one cause

A condenser water loop serves the whole building, and the common-area equipment is a small fraction of what hangs on it. Every private unit sits on the same water.

So when an owner’s own contractor replaces a part and the same fault returns a month later, the cause is usually not inside the unit. It is in the plant, and nobody was watching it.

We do not work inside private units. Our loop work stops at the branch valve.

04 — Approach

Why we do not sell a generic schedule

Half our frequencies are higher than the standard asks. That is not padding — it is what a coastal roof does to equipment.

Salt air

An oceanfront roof corrodes fasteners, fan hardware and panel connections faster than any inland site.

Cooling all year

The plant never gets an off season, so there is no quiet month to defer work into.

Hurricanes

Pre-season readiness before 1 June. Named-storm securing, and a post-storm restart that tests before it runs.

The questions you are going to ask

Am I liable if there is a Legionella case?

The association carries the risk; documentation is what protects it. We run the water program to an ASHRAE 188 written plan, with sampling before any remediation dose and a retest at least 48 hours after normal operation resumes. Every test and every corrective action is kept in your portal, dated.

Are you licensed and insured?

Florida CMC licence, general liability and workers’ compensation certificates are attached to the written proposal. We do not print licence numbers on a web page.

Will my residents notice?

Routine work is done in the plant rooms and on the roof. Anything needing a water shutdown, a lift booking or amenity access is scheduled with you in advance and in writing.

Does the program need anything from my staff?

Three small recurring tasks, and we would rather say so now than surprise you later: a weekly tower walk-by, a weekly check that every leg sees flow, and a biweekly mini-split filter wash. If your staff cannot take them, we price them in.

What happens to the wash water?

It is tested and never sent to a storm drain. Property managers get fined for this; it is in our scope so that you do not.

Who handles the boiler certificate?

We do. Florida Statute 554.108 requires a certificate inspection every two years and an internal every four. We prepare the boilers and coordinate the authorised inspector. It is a legal obligation that sits with the building, and it is easy to miss.

How do I know you are better than the contractor I have?

Compare the monthly deliverable. Ask your current contractor for the last twelve months of trended readings on any one piece of equipment.
