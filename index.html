Who we are

Technicians, not a call centre

Curious Mechanical Services installs, services and maintains the central HVAC plant of commercial and residential buildings across South Florida — chillers and cooling towers, boilers and pumps, rooftop units, heat pump loops, the air side and the controls that tie them together.

The person who answers the phone has walked your plant. Every visit leaves a reading behind, and every failure leaves a written cause. That is the whole company in two sentences.

EPA 608 certified · UA Local 725 / ARPEC trained · work to ASHRAE 180 and ASHRAE 188.

Mission

Find the cause, write it down, prove the fix

To close every call with a cause rather than a repair. A fault that is fixed but not explained is a fault that comes back, and comes back billable.

So we measure before we touch anything, we write what we found, and we take the reading that proves the repair held.

Vision

Maintenance judged by evidence, not by signatures

A market where a maintenance contract is judged by the readings it produces, not by the checklist it signs. Where a building’s own numbers decide what gets serviced and when.

We want to be the contractor a property manager keeps because the failures stopped repeating — and can point at the trend that shows it.
