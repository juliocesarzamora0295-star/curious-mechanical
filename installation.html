01 — Installation

Installed so it can be maintained

Most maintenance problems are installation problems nobody wrote down.

  1. Load calculation first

    Manual N and Manual CS before anything is ordered. Never what the truck had.

  2. Coordinated before a pipe is cut

    Routing, clearances, service access and hangers agreed with the other trades.

  3. Passivated before it runs

    Hydrostatic test, flush, chemical clean, neutralize, passivate. Then balanced, with an independent TAB report.

  4. Handed over with its numbers

    As-builts, equipment IDs, QR tags and start-up readings. The building owns the data, not us.
